THE PIPELINE

One platform, five stages

Every candidate moves through the same disciplined sequence — from raw biological signal to a molecule ready for the bench.

01

Signal Mining

Ingest omics, literature and assay data to surface disease-relevant targets.

02

Target Scoring

Rank targets by druggability, novelty and safety signal in one model.

03

Generative Design

Propose candidate molecules constrained by binding and ADMET criteria.
